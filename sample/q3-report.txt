Q3 Business Review. Revenue grew 18% quarter over quarter to 42 million dollars, driven by enterprise renewals. Gross margin improved to 71% after we moved inference workloads to reserved capacity. We added 1,240 new customers, of which 86 are enterprise accounts. The mobile launch slipped by three weeks due to an app-store review issue. Cloud cost per customer fell 12% after the storage migration. The biggest risk next quarter is concentration: our top five customers now account for 31% of revenue. Hiring remained flat with 4 open roles in security and platform. Churn held steady at 1.9% monthly.
